# Guessaday privacy policy

Guessaday does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The app works entirely offline. It makes no network requests.
- Your settings (date range, timer, display options) are stored only on your
  device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that
  collect data.
- The only outgoing links are the ones you tap yourself in the "About" dialog,
  which open in your browser or email app.

If you have questions, contact Moritz Firsching at moritz.firsching@gmail.com.
